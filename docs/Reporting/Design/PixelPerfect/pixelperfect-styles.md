# Styles in PixelPerfect

Standardize and polish the presentation of your report with PixelPerfect styles. Styles are reusable sets of properties for content that allow you to achieve a consistent look and feel for your report.

## Creating a new style

Do the following:

1. Open the Properties for an item where styles can be defined.
2. Expand Styles.
3. In the Style dropdown, click Create a New Style.
4. Expand the Style, and define a Name.
5. Customize the properties in the style, such as font, backgrounds, borders, and padding.
