# Adding external images to the report template

You can add images from external sources, such as your computer or a web URL, to your PixelPerfect template.

For information about adding images of Qlik Sense application content to your report template, see [*Adding images to the report template*](pixelperfect-add-images.htm).

## Adding an external image

You can add images from your computer or an external URL to your report. For example, you can add your company logo to the top of the report.

### From your computer

Do the following:

1. From the toolbox panel on the left side of the designer, drag a ![Select image](../../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_image_outline.svg) Picture Box onto the design surface.

   For more information about navigating the PixelPerfect designer, see [*Navigating the PixelPerfect designer*](pixelperfect-designer.htm).
2. The new Picture Box should be selected. Click the gear icon that appears next to the object.
3. Under Image Source in the popup menu, click the ellipsis (three dots) icon.
4. Select an image from your computer.

### From a URL

Do the following:

1. From the toolbox panel on the left side of the designer, drag a ![Select image](../../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_image_outline.svg) Picture Box onto the design surface.
2. The new Picture Box should be selected. Click the gear icon that appears next to the object.
3. Under Image URL in the popup menu, insert the URL.
