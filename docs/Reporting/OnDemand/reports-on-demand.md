# Working with on-demand reporting

With on-demand reports, you can download reports directly from your web browser while analyzing an application. The data in on-demand reports is based on your current selections in the application, as well as your access level to the application. Report developers control which templates are usable for on-demand reporting.

## Configuring on-demand reporting

Report developers configure on-demand reporting for specific [report templates](#glossary-0 "Glossary term"). When a template is activated for on-demand reporting, users can generate reports as they analyze an application in sheet view.

[*Configuring on-demand reporting*](reports-on-demand-configure.htm)

## Creating on-demand reports

Application consumers generate on-demand reports as they navigate through the sheets in an application.

[*Creating on-demand reports*](reports-on-demand-create.htm)
