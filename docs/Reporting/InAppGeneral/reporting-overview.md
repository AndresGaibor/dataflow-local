# Reporting

In the Reporting section of the application, you can configure in-application reporting. With in-application reporting, configure and generate dynamic enterprise reports using your Qlik Sense application data and visualizations. Build [templates](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") to define report structure, and then configure [report tasks](#glossary-1 "Glossary term"), filtering, and distribution settings from directly within the application. You can also activate on-demand reporting to allow users to generate reports while analyzing sheets in the application.

Click Reporting under the Prepare tab in the navigation bar to open the Reporting section.

For full details about in-application reporting, see [*In-application reporting with Qlik Cloud Analytics*](cloud-in-app-reports-intro.htm).
