# Lasso selection

When you want to make a draw or lasso selection, you must first click inside the visualization and turn on lasso selection by clicking the lasso icon ![Lasso](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_lasso.svg) at the top of the visualization. On a computer, you can also press Shift and make the selection.

You draw a freehand circle to capture and select data points.

Selection of values made in a scatter plot using lasso selection.

![Scatter plot with lasso selection.](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/ui_gen_LoopSelection.png "Click to view full size")

Your lasso selections only include visible data points. For charts using a continuous axis, data points not visible in the chart will be excluded, even if they are within the area being selected.
