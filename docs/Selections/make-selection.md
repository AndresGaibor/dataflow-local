# Exploring data with visualizations

You make selections by clicking and drawing lines in the different visualizations.

When you make a selection, all associated visualizations are updated immediately to reflect the selection. You confirm the selection by clicking ![ ](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_tick.svg), or by clicking anywhere on the sheet outside the visualization, including in another visualization, (in which case you generate a new selection). You can also press Enter to confirm.

You cancel a selection by clicking ![Cancel](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_remove.svg). You can also press Esc to undo.

By default, new selections in a visualization are added to the previous ones. You deselect an item by clicking it. On a computer, you can hold down Ctrl while you make a selection, to automatically clear previous selections in a visualization, and only keep the new selection.

Information noteWhen making selections in filter panes there is a difference between Cancel selection (![Cancel](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_remove.svg)) and Clear selection (![Selections - Clear all](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_clear_selections2.svg)). With ![Cancel](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_remove.svg), you only clear the latest selection, but ![Selections - Clear all](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_clear_selections2.svg) clears all selections.
