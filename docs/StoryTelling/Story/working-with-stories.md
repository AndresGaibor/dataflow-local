# Working with stories

In this section you will find how to work with [stories](#glossary-0 "Glossary term"). For instance, you can understand how to create, duplicate and change settings for a story.

## Creating new stories

Do the following:

1. From the application overview, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg) to view the stories

   From the storytelling view, in Assets, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_play.svg) Stories.
2. Click Create new story.

   A new story is created, with the title My new story.
3. Give your story a meaningful title and add a description, if you want.
4. Click outside the text area to save the title and description.

The new story is saved.

## Editing stories

When you load data into an application you analyze the data to get new insights. You can present your new insights in a new story or in a edited version of an existing story. For more information, see [*Loading and managing data with Data Manager*](../../LoadData/managing-data.htm) and [*Loading and transforming data with scripting*](../../Scripting/introduction-data-modeling.htm).

A snapshot's state and selections will not be updated at a data reload. It will always reflect the data that existed at the point in time the snapshot was taken. However, live data [sheets](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") are not static and will be affected by a data reload.

You can replace a snapshot of a visualization on a slide by going to its source, in the application. The visualization will now have updated data. You can take a new snapshot to use in your story.

When you take a snapshot you can make an annotation for your snapshot. The annotation helps you distinguish between the different snapshots in the snapshot library when you build your story. The annotation is not visible when you play the story.

Tip noteIf you want to keep the original story, make a duplicate of it before you start editing.

Tip noteYou can download the story if you want to present it outside of Qlik Sense.

### Changing the titles and descriptions of stories

You can change the title and description of your stories.

Note the following:

- To change the name or description of a story, the application must be in your personal cloud or group workspace.

#### Changing titles and descriptions from the application overview

Do the following:

1. In the application overview, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg) Stories.
2. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Details.
3. Click Edit details.
4. Edit Title and Description.
5. Click Done.

#### Changing titles and descriptions from the storytelling view

Do the following:

1. In the storytelling view, click Assets.
2. Click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_play.svg)Stories.
3. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Details.
4. Click Edit details.
5. Edit Title and Description.
6. Click Done.

### Changing the thumbnails of stories

You can replace the default thumbnail of a story with another thumbnail, to make it easier to distinguish between stories in the application overview and in the story navigator. You can use one of the default images, or an image of your own.

#### Changing thumbnails in the application overview

Do the following:

1. In the application overview, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg) Stories.
2. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Details.
3. Click Edit details.
4. Click ![Select image](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_image_outline.svg) on the thumbnail.

   The Media library opens.
5. Select the image you want to use as a thumbnail for the story and click Insert.
6. Click Done.

#### Changing the thumbnails in the storytelling view

Do the following:

1. In the storytelling view, click Assets.
2. Click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_play.svg)Stories.
3. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Details.
4. Click Edit details.
5. Click ![Select image](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_image_outline.svg) on the thumbnail.

   The Media library opens.
6. Click on a folder in the media library, for example In app or Default.
7. Select the image you want to use as a thumbnail for the story and click Insert.
8. Click Done.

Tip noteThe optimal aspect ratio of a thumbnail is 8:5 (width:height).

The following formats are supported: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, and .gif.When using Qlik Sense in Qlik Cloud, you upload images to the In app folder in the media library.

## Duplicating stories

You can duplicate any story, regardless of whether it belongs to the application or if you created it yourself. The purpose of duplicating stories is to save time by reusing content, and to modify the duplicate to fit your needs.

A duplicated story contains the same content as the original story, and is linked to the same snapshots. The duplicated story will not be updated if the original story is updated. Duplicated stories appear under My stories in application overview and in the story navigator.

### Duplicating a story from application overview

Do the following:

1. Click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg) on the left-hand side to show the stories of the application.
2. Right-click a story.

   The shortcut menu opens.
3. Click Duplicate.

The new story is created. It is located under My stories.

### Duplicating a story from the storytelling view

Do the following:

1. In the storytelling view, click Assets.
2. Click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_play.svg)Stories.
3. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Details.
4. Right-click a story and select Duplicate.

The new story is created. It is located under My own.

Tip noteYou can also duplicate a story when you are in storytelling view, using the story navigator ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg).

## Deleting stories

Do the following:

1. In the application overview, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_slideshow_outline.svg) to view the stories.

   Alternatively, from the storytelling view, click ![Story Mode](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_play.svg)Stories.
2. Click ![More](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the story and select Delete.
3. Click Delete, to confirm that you want to delete the story.
