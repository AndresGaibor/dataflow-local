# Building stories

The purpose of a [story](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") is to gather insight and build narratives from your data. You structure the story to convince your audience, by taking [snapshots](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") of your visualizations and put them in slides. To make your slides stand out you can apply visual effects, add text and shapes, and can embed [sheets](#glossary-2 "Glossary term") to make your story interactive. A story is connected to its application, so you can return to the live data anytime, to discover new and hidden stories.
