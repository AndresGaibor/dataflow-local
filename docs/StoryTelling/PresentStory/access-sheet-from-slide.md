# Accessing a sheet from a slide

When you play a [story](#glossary-0 "Glossary term"), you can navigate from a live data [sheet](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") to the sheet in sheet view. By doing this you get access to the original sheet with your selections applied to it. You can then make new selections to take your story in new directions.

Do the following:

1. While playing the story, navigate to a story slide with a live data sheet.
2. Click Go to sheet.

You are directed to the sheet in sheet view carrying with you the selections you have made on the live data sheet.
