# Selecting in a live data sheet

When you play a [story](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") you can make [selections](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") in live data [sheet](#glossary-2 "Glossary term") just as you can in a sheet in sheet view.

## Enable selections

Do the following:

1. In storytelling view, click ![Arrow right](../../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_triangle_right.svg) above the timeline.
2. Navigate to a slide with a live data sheet.
3. Click on the sheet to enable it for selections.

   The sheet is possible to interact with.
4. Start making selections in the [visualizations](#glossary-3 "Glossary term").

The visualizations respond to selections and filter out subsets of the data.

Information noteYou disable the possibility to make selections by clicking outside the sheet, or by navigating to another story slide.

When you are playing a story on a small screen you must go to sheet view to view the sheet and make selections.

## Reset selections

You can reset the selections you have made on the live data sheet.

Do the following:

- While playing the story and with some selections made on a live data sheet, click Reset selections.

The sheet's selections will be reset to the same selections it had at the time you inserted the live data sheet on the slide.

Information noteThe reset gives you the possibility to always return to the same selections as when you inserted the live data sheet on the slide.
