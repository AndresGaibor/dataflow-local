# Understanding your data with the catalog

Catalog is your tool to explore all the assets you have access to in your available spaces. You can access the Catalog page from the left menu.

You can browse your datasets in the catalog. Datasets can be the result of a manual upload, or created from a data project task when using the Publish to catalog option. For more information, see [*Browsing datasets from the Catalog*](../DataProducts/Browsing-datasets-from-catalog.htm).

The catalog will have additional tools available for your data depending on your subscription.

## [*Assessing data quality*](../DataProducts/Data-quality-and-data-discovery.htm)

When looking at a dataset or a data product, you can get an idea of the quality of the data they contain, from the overall quality and freshness, to the number of empty or invalid row for each field of a dataset.

## [*Data freshness*](../DataProducts/Data-freshness.htm)

The data freshness indicates the last update of the data source.

## [*Qlik Trust Score™*](../DataProducts/Qlik-Trust-score.htm)

The Qlik Trust Score™ helps you answer the question "How trustable is my dataset?". This global quality indicator aggregates several metrics into a single and easy-to-understand score.

## [*Working with data products*](../DataProducts/Data-products.htm)

Seamlessly access and utilize data products built and activated on the data integration side. View comprehensive details, including descriptions, purpose, and contact information for inquiries, supported by related documentation and quality metrics.

## [*Understanding your data with catalog tools*](../../Catalog/catalog-overview.htm)

Read more about all catalog tools in Qlik Cloud that help you improve your data efficiency while maintaining security and compliance standards.

## [*Working with business glossaries*](../../Catalog/BusinessGlossary/Working-business-glossaries.htm)

Business glossaries help you to standardize terms and definitions. They consist of logical categories populated with terms described in clear language that everyone across an organization can refer to and understand.
