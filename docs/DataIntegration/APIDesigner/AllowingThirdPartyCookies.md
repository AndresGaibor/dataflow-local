# Allowing third-party cookies in your browser

API Contracts relies on a third-party
provider (Auth0) for security and user account management, so third-party cookies should
be enabled.

Open your browser edit the settings to allow third-party cookies:

- On Google Chrome, go to Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies and disable the Block third-party cookies
  option.
- On Mozilla Firefox, go to Options > Privacy & Security > History (or Preferences > Privacy & Security > History on Mac OS) and select Remember
  history.
- On Safari, go to Safari > Preferences > Privacy panel and clear the Prevent cross-site
  tracking check box.
- On Internet Explorer, go to Internet Options > Privacy > Advanced, select Accept under
  Third-party cookies and click
  OK.
- On Microsoft Edge, go to Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and data stored > Manage and delete cookies and site data and disable the Block third-party cookies
  option.
