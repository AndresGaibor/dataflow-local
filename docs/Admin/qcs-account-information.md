# Finding tenant information

View key details about your Qlik Cloud tenant for support queries, troubleshooting, or subscription management.

## Finding tenant information in the About window

Do the following:

1. Click your user profile icon in the upper-right corner.
2. Select About from the menu.

A window shows the following tenant information:

- Tenant alias hostname
- Tenant hostname
- Tenant ID
- Qlik Cloud ID
- Subscription ID
- Subscription period
- Recovery address

## Identifying your tenant region from the tenant hostname

Your tenant hostname includes a region identifier. The region identifier appears after the tenant name in the tenant URL (for example, eu or us).

For details and a complete mapping of region identifiers to regions, see [*Creating and configuring your tenant*](../Introduction/qcs-tenant-domain.htm).

## Finding subscription plan information

To find out which subscription plan (pricing plan, tier, or edition) your organization is on, ask your service account owner (SAO) to check in [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/). In the subscription overview, the SAO can see the current plan and subscription type.

To learn more about available pricing plans, see [Qlik Pricing](https://www.qlik.com/us/pricing).

## Checking which capabilities are enabled in your tenant

The Capabilities list in Administration > Home shows which capabilities are enabled for your subscription and the limits for each capability.

For more information, see [*Monitoring resource consumption*](mc-monitor-consumption.htm).
