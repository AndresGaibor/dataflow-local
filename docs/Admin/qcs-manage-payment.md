# Managing Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS and Qlik Sense Business subscriptions

As the service account owner (SAO), you can manage certain aspects of your Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS or Qlik Sense Business subscription from My Qlik.

## Managing Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS subscriptions

With a Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS subscription, you can do the following in My Qlik:

- Review payment and billing information.
- View and download invoices.
- Manage payment methods.

For subscription changes, such as renewal, contact your Qlik account manager.

## Managing Qlik Sense Business subscriptions

With a Qlik Sense Business subscription, you can do the following in My Qlik:

- Review payment and billing information.
- View and download invoices.
- Manage payment methods.
- Renew your subscription.
- Adjust the number of users.
- Cancel your subscription.

Information noteIf you did not purchase your subscription directly from Qlik, you won’t be able to manage your subscription in My Qlik. The third-party that you purchased through can manage your subscription for you.

### Upgrading a Qlik Sense Business subscription

You can upgrade a Qlik Sense Business subscription to a capacity-based Qlik Cloud Analytics subscription. To upgrade, contact your Qlik account manager.

### Renewing a Qlik Sense Business subscription

Do the following:

1. Go to [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/) and select the subscription.
2. Click Renew.
3. Click + or – to specify the number of paid users for the renewal period.
   - The monthly cost based on tiered pricing is displayed to the right.
   - You cannot reduce the number of seats below the number of active users in the [tenant](#glossary-0 "Glossary term"). Remove users first and wait at least two minutes before proceeding.
4. Click Continue.

   The payment page appears.
5. To use the credit card on file, ensure that it is selected.

   Tip noteTo add a new credit card, click Add new payment and enter your new credit card information.
6. Click Continue.

   The Overview page displays your selections.
7. Confirm your selection and click Pay.

   Once the payment is processed, your subscription is renewed.

### Reducing the number of users

When you reduce the number of users, the changes will take effect at the beginning of the next subscription period. Until then, your current seats remain available. You can undo a reduction at any time before the end of the period.

Do the following:

1. Go to [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/) and select the subscription.
2. Click Manage.
3. Click Reduce seats.
4. Click + or – to specify the number of paid users for the next subscription period.
   - The monthly cost based on tiered pricing is displayed to the right.
   - You cannot reduce the number of seats below the number of active users in the [tenant](#glossary-1 "Glossary term"). Remove users first and wait at least two minutes before proceeding.
5. Click Continue.
6. Review the confirmation dialog, select I Understand, and click Confirm.

   The subscription tile updates to show the requested reduction and the effective date.

#### Undoing a seat reduction

Do the following:

1. In My Qlik, find the subscription and click Undo Request.
2. Select the I Understand checkbox and click Confirm.

   The subscription tile updates to reflect the original number of seats.

### Canceling a Qlik Sense Business subscription

You can cancel your subscription in My Qlik. The cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid term. You can undo the cancellation any time before the end of the term.

Do the following:

1. Go to [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/) and select the subscription.
2. Click Manage.
3. Click Cancel subscription.
4. Review the confirmation dialog, select I Understand, and click Confirm.

## Cancellation, access, and refunds

The following information applies to digital customer subscriptions for Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS and Qlik Sense Business.

### Cancellation and access

Canceling a subscription does not immediately remove access. You can continue using the software until the end of the current paid term.

### Refunds

Qlik does not provide refunds or credits for canceled subscriptions. This includes partial refunds or credits for any remaining time in a billing cycle.

When you purchase Qlik software, you agree to the applicable terms of use. The following purchase agreements state that no refunds or credits of prepaid fees are granted upon termination or expiration of a subscription:

## Managing payment methods

With a Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS or Qlik Sense Business subscription, you can update your credit card information in My Qlik.

### Adding a credit card

Do the following:

1. In [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/), go to the Payment & billing page.
2. Under Payment methods, click Add credit card.
3. Enter your credit card payment information and click Continue.

The new credit card appears under Payment methods.

### Deleting a credit card

You cannot delete the default payment method. Set another payment method as default before deleting the existing one.

Do the following:

1. In My Qlik, go to the Payment & billing page.
2. Under Payment methods, click ![More](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the credit card that you wish to remove.
3. Select Delete.
4. On the Delete credit card? page click Delete to remove the payment method.

### Changing the default payment method

Do the following:

1. In My Qlik, go to the Payment & billing page.
2. Under Payment methods click ![More](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) on the credit card that you wish to set as the default.
3. Select Set as default payment method.
