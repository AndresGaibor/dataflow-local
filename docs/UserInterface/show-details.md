# Viewing details for visualizations

View the data used to make a [visualization](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") and their dependencies and linked glossary terms in Details.

You can view the fields and master items used to make the dimension and measures in a visualization in Details. You can open details by clicking ![Show details](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_info_outline.svg) on a visualization or by right-clicking a visualization and selecting ![Show details](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_info_outline.svg) Show details.

Details shows the [dimensions](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") and [measure](#glossary-2 "Glossary term") used to make a visualization.

Details, showing the dimension and measure of a visualization.

![Details for a chart, showing the field City as the Dimension and the master measure Gross margin, with a linked glossary definition, as the Measure](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/ui_ShowDetails.png "Click to view full size")

Master items used as dimensions or measures may have linked glossary terms set as definitions or related terms. Hovering your cursor over the term shows the term's definition. You can click the term to open the term in the glossary. For more information on glossaries and master items, see [*Viewing master items and linked terms*](../Catalog/BusinessGlossary/business-glossaries-master-items.htm#Viewing).

Master item in Details with linked glossary term

![Image of detailed information of a glossary item](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/ui_businessGlossary_master_measure_details.png "Click to view full size")

To view the source fields and datasets for the data, click Show dependencies. This shows the source fields for dimensions and measures along with their source [dataset](#glossary-3 "Glossary term") or [connections](#glossary-4 "Glossary term")

Details, showing dependencies

![Image of dependencies showing source fields](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/ui_ShowDetailsDependencies.png "Click to view full size")

You can view lineage information for the field by clicking ![More](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) and selecting Lineage. For more information on lineage, see [*Analyzing lineage in Analytics*](../Catalog/lineage.htm).
