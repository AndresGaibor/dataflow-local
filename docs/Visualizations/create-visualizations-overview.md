# Creating and editing visualizations

You create [visualizations](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") from predefined [charts](#glossary-1 "Glossary term"), fields, or custom objects. Once added to your [sheet](#glossary-2 "Glossary term"), you can edit and refine your visualizations.

Your visualization design is guided by the data in your fields and the unlimited potential for exploring associations and correlations through [measures](#glossary-3 "Glossary term") and [dimensions](#glossary-4 "Glossary term").

Get started here: [*Creating visualizations*](create-visualizations.htm).

After creating a visualization, you may want to make adjustments to improve how it conveys information to users. For example, you can change the data used, or adjust the appearance of the visualization. You can add more dimensions or measures for further depth of information or remove some to improve clarity. Learn more about editing your visualizations here: [*Editing visualizations*](edit-visualizations.htm).

Adherence to design principals and clear communication of information is key to creation of applications that engage and invite data discovery. All skill levels will benefit from reviewing best practices for designing visualizations. See [*Best practices for designing visualizations*](create-viz-best-practices.htm).
