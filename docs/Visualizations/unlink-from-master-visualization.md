# Unlinking from a master visualization

Unlink a visualization from its master [visualization](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") to edit only the current instance. Unlinking removes the connection between the visualization on your sheet and the master item. The current visualization becomes independent. To edit all visualizations that are linked to a master visualization, see [*Editing a master visualization*](../Assets/edit-master-visualization.htm).

Do the following:

1. Click ![](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_edit_outline.svg) Edit sheet.
2. Click ![More](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_more.svg) in the top right corner of a linked visualization, and then click Unlink visualization.

   A dialog is displayed that you are about to unlink from a master visualization.
3. Click OK.

The visualization on the sheet can now be edited and the changes will not affect any other visualizations.

Tip noteYou can also right-click the linked visualization, and then click Unlink visualization from the shortcut menu.
