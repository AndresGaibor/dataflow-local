# Video player

The video player (Video player) lets you play videos in different formats from external sources.

Information noteVideo player is included as a part of the Dashboard bundle. Dashboard bundle objects have additional limitations and may not have the same support as other visualizations. For more information, see [*Dashboard bundle*](dashboard-bundle.htm).

The following video formats are supported. For more information, see [*Video player*](#LimitationsVP).

- MP4
- OGG
- WEBM
- YouTube

URLs used in the video player must be added to the allowlist by the tenant admin in the Administration [activity center](#glossary-0 "Glossary term").
 For more information, see [*Managing Content Security Policy (CSP)*](../../Admin/mc-administer-content-security-policy.htm). For allowlist examples, see [*Video player*](#vpallowlistexamples).

## When to use the video player

The video player is useful when you want to include a video in your application. For example, you want to show a demo or a training video, add a tutorial, or present corporate news.
