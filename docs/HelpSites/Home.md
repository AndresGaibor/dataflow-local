## Building automations

Qlik Automate provides a no-code visual interface that helps you easily build automated analytics and data workflows.

An automation is a sequence of actions and triggers that runs like a program. It can be a simple workflow that collects information from one application and passes it to another, or it can be an end-to-end pipeline that takes you from raw data to active intelligence. Qlik Automate lets you automate your analytics environment, create data-driven workflows, and embed data and analytics into your business processes.

[See all topics](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_QlikAutomation/introduction/home-automation.htm)

## Administration

Administrators are responsible for deploying, configuring, and managing the Qlik Cloud subscription and environment. The Qlik Cloud environment offers centralized management and governance and helps to ensure user adoption, accuracy, and system reliability.

![](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Content/Resources/Images/QlikIcons2024/Checklist.svg)

### [Planning your Qlik Cloud deployment](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/Introduction/planning-deployment.htm)

To successfully plan your Qlik Cloud deployment you will need to consider factors like your company's geographical distribution, existing deployments, security, capacity, and how you wish to manage your Qlik Sense subscription and environment.

![](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Content/Resources/Images/QlikIcons2024/Cloud-Accelerated.svg)

### [Deploying Qlik Cloud](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/Introduction/qcs-for-owners-and-admins.htm)

To deploy Qlik Cloud, follow a standard set of high-level steps, including registering, configuring the system, adding users, and setting up management processes.

![](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Content/Resources/Images/QlikIcons2024/Cloud-Computing.svg)
