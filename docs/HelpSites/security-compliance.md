# Qlik Cloud security, compliance, and privacy

Qlik adheres to a variety of international compliance and privacy standards. For a complete list, see [Trust and security at Qlik](https://www.qlik.com/us/trust).

Warning noteSeveral assets in Qlik Cloud, such as scripts, table recipes and data flows can be potentially used to execute malicious script. When running or refreshing an imported or shared asset, it will be run with your credentials, and you are responsible for ensuring that it meets the security guidelines of your organization. Make sure your trust the source of your asset before refreshing or running it.

## Qlik Cloud Government compliance certifications and attestations

Qlik Cloud Government has been purpose built for the U.S. Public Sector and designed to meet the security and compliance standards of multiple levels and branches of U.S. government, including the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP), the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA), and Government RAMP (GovRAMP, d.b.a StateRAMP). These authorizations bodies provide a standardized approach to security authorizations for Cloud Service Offerings, accelerating cloud adoption across the U.S. Public Sector.

- Criminal Justice Information Services: Qlik Cloud Government and Qlik Cloud Government - DoD have completed an independent third-party attestation confirming that our government regions meet the U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation's Criminal Justice Information Services control requirements. The attestation evaluate the suitability of the design and operating effectiveness of Qlik's internal controls related to the protection of CJIS information. By using [Customer Managed Keys](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/Admin/customer-managed-keys.htm) along with features and tools in Qlik Cloud Government and Qlik Cloud Government - DoD, customers can host CJIS data. Qlik will enter CJIS agreements with relevant public safety agencies.
- Department of Defense Status: Qlik Cloud Government has achieved the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) IL 2 and DoD IL4 Authorizations. The IL4 Authorization applies to Qlik Cloud Government - DoD only.
  For more information, see the [DoD Cloud Security Portal](https://public.cyber.mil/dccs/).
- FedRAMP Status: Qlik Cloud Government has achieved FedRAMP's authorized designation at the Moderate Impact Level (IL). It is listed in the Marketplace under Authorized products.
  For more information see the [FedRAMP Marketplace](https://marketplace.fedramp.gov/#!/product/qlik-cloud-government).
- GovRAMP (d.b.a StateRAMP) Status: Qlik Cloud Government has achieved the Government Risk and Authorization Management Program (GovRAMP) Moderate Authorized status. GovRAMP simplifies security for U.S. State, Local, and Higher Education organizations by providing a standardized approach to security authorizations for Cloud Service Providers.
  For more information, see [GovRAMP](https://govramp.org/ "Link to govramp.org").
- HIPAA Status: Qlik Cloud Government and Qlik Cloud Government - DoD have obtained independent third-party attestation confirming that our government regions meet the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) which is a set of national standards to protect sensitive patient health information from being disclosed without the patient’s consent or knowledge. The attestation evaluates the suitability of the design and operating effectiveness of Qlik’s internal controls related to the protection of Personal Health Information (PHI) and US HIPAA regulatory requirements. By using [Customer Managed Keys](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/Admin/customer-managed-keys.htm) along with features and tools in Qlik Cloud Government and Qlik Cloud Government - DoD, customers can host PHI as defined in HIPAA. Customers must also enter into a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Qlik.
- ITAR Status: Qlik Cloud Government and Qlik Cloud Government - DoD are compliant with the United States International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) for the handling of software and technical data controlled on the United States Munitions List (USML). Qlik Cloud Government provides an environment that is physically located in the U.S. and access to the environment is restricted to U.S. Persons thereby allowing qualified companies to use Qlik Cloud Government to transmit, process, and store protected articles and data subject to ITAR restrictions.
  For more information, see [Directorate of Defense Trade Controls](https://www.pmddtc.state.gov/ddtc_public/ddtc_public?id=ddtc_kb_article_page&sys_id=24d528fddbfc930044f9ff621f961987).
- TX-RAMP Status: Qlik Cloud Government has achieved Texas Risk and Authorization Management Program (TX-RAMP) Level 2 Authorization supporting confidential agency data determined to be at the moderate or high impact level. The Texas Department of Information Resources (DIR) provides a standardized approach for security assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring of cloud computing services that process the data of a state agency.

  For more information, see [TX-RAMP](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp).

## Qlik Cloud and HIPAA

The US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) is a set of national standards to protect sensitive patient health information from being disclosed without the patient’s consent or knowledge. Qlik has successfully completed a SOC2 Type 2 + HITRUST attestation for Qlik Cloud. The attestation evaluates the suitability of the design and operating effectiveness of Qlik’s internal controls related to the protection of Personal Health Information (PHI) and US HIPAA regulatory requirements. By using [Customer Managed Keys](/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Sense_Hub/Admin/customer-managed-keys.htm) along with features and tools in Qlik Cloud, customers can host PHI as defined in HIPAA. Customers must also enter into a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Qlik.

For more information, see [Healthcare analytics](https://www.qlik.com/us/solutions/industries/healthcare-analytics#hipaa)

Information noteQlik Sense Business does not support the use of Customer Managed Keys.

## Qlik Cloud and German C5 (C5:2020 Type 2)

C5 (Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue) is a cloud security attestation framework published by the German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI). Under Section 393 SGB V of Germany's Digital Act, processing of German health data by cloud-computing services requires providers to meet the German C5 Type 2 standard.

Qlik has obtained an independent third-party attestation for C5:2020 Type 2. Type 2 attestation provides assurance that relevant cloud security controls are evaluated for both design and operating effectiveness over a defined review period, rather than at a single point in time. The attestation is based on the C5:2020 version of the standard. Updates to newer C5 versions are reviewed as part of the regular annual audit cycle.

For more information, see:[Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue - C5:2020](https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/EN/BSI/CloudComputing/ComplianceControlsCatalogue/2020/C5_2020.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=4)
