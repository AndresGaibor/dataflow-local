# Qlik Cloud Government overview

Qlik Cloud is available in multiple editions to support a wide range of customer requirements, including the U.S. Public sector. The Qlik Cloud Government offering is available for the U.S. Public sector and some U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) agencies. The Qlik Cloud Government - DoD offering is available only for the U.S. Department of Defense.

These government editions differ from Qlik’s commercial Qlik Cloud offering due to the security protocols required to support the different constituencies. For a summary of compliance certifications and attestations, see [Qlik Cloud security, compliance, and privacy](security-compliance.htm). Qlik keeps platform functionality aligned across commercial and government environments wherever possible.

Each of the Qlik Cloud Government offerings can support onboarding a restricted subset of commercial entities:

- Qlik Cloud Government is available for United States Federal, State, Local and Tribal governments, educational institutions, and contractors holding or processing data on behalf of the U.S. State, Federal, or DoD governmental agencies
- Qlik Cloud Government - DoD is available for agencies of the U. S. DoD, restricted subsets of Federal agencies, and DoD contractors.

Contact your Qlik representative, PAM, or OEM/ISV representative for more information.
