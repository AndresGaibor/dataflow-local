# Collaborating with other users

The Qlik Cloud Analytics [activity centers](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") are a collaborative environment for developing and sharing content.

Use shared spaces to develop and share content with other members of your Qlik Cloud tenant.
You can also share your personal applications with other members. Use notes to capture and share insights.

### [*Capturing and sharing insights using Notes*](../Notes/notes-overview.htm)

Create notes to capture data insights and collaborate with others.

### [*Working in spaces*](Spaces.htm)

You can collaborate and share applications with other users in spaces. Shared spaces enable the collaborative development of applications and controlled access to applications in the space. Managed spaces enable governed access to applications in that space.

### [*Granting access to sheets, sheet groups, bookmarks, and stories*](../Share/make-public-or-private.htm)

You can make your sheets, bookmarks, and stories public so users with access to your application can view them.
