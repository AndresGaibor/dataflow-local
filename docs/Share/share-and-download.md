# Sharing and downloading application content

You can share insights from your apps. You can download [sheets](#glossary-0 "Glossary term") and [visualizations](#glossary-1 "Glossary term") as PDFs and images. You can also download the data in a visualization as an xlsx file.

You can share links to visualizations with custom selection states, enabling you to share initial insights quickly with other Qlik Cloud members.
You can also email visualizations to others, enabling insights to be shared with users who may not have access to Qlik Cloud.

To share and download, you will need to do the following tasks:

- [Download application content](../Download/downloading-visualization.htm)
- [Share visualizations](sharing-visualizations.htm)
- [Share links to bookmarks](sharing-bookmarks.htm)
