# Web storage providers

The Qlik Web Storage Provider Connectors allow you to seamlessly connect to your file-based data stored on a web storage provider, such as Dropbox, and stream that data into your Qlik Sense application.

## Storage providers

The following storage providers are supported:

- [*Amazon S3*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Amazon-S3-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Amazon S3 V2*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Amazon-S3-V2-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Azure Storage*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Azure-Storage-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Dropbox*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Dropbox-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*File (via Direct Access gateway)*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/File-DG-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Google Cloud Storage*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Google-Cloud-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Google Drive*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Google-Drive-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*Office 365 Sharepoint*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/Office365-Sharepoint-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*OneDrive*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/OneDrive-storage-provider-connector.htm)
- [*SFTP*](../Storage%20provider%20connectors/SFTP-storage-provider-connector.htm)

## Version history

### 4.32.0

As of May 2023:

- The SFTP connector is available in Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.

### 4.31.0

As of February 2023:

- The SFTP connector is available in Qlik Cloud.

### 4.28.0

As of December 2022:

- The Amazon S3 V2 connector is available in Qlik Cloud. Qlik recommends that you use the Amazon S3 V2 connector when developing new applications.

### 4.18.0

As of May 2022:

- The Amazon S3 connector is available on Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.
- The Azure Storage connector is available on Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.
- The Google Cloud Storage connector is available on Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.
- The Office 365 Sharepoint connector is available on Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.
- The OneDrive connector is available on Qlik Sense Enterprise on Windows and Qlik Sense Desktop.

### 4.3.5
