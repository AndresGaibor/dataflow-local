# Registering for a subscription

Qlik Cloud is hosted and managed by Qlik. You can choose between the subscription options for Qlik Cloud Analytics and Qlik Talend Cloud and register for a subscription. After registering, follow the administrator steps to set up an identity provider and manage the tenant.

## Starting a trial

To start a trial, follow these steps:

1. Visit [Qlik Free Trials](https://www.qlik.com/us/free-trial).
2. Select the Qlik Talend Cloud or Qlik Cloud Analytics trial and follow the on-screen instructions.

During the trial, the service account owner (the user who created the tenant) can see how many days remain, extend the trial once before it expires, or purchase a full subscription.

## Registering for a Qlik Cloud subscription

To register for a subscription directly (without a trial):

- For Qlik Cloud Analytics or Qlik Talend Cloud subscriptions – Visit [Qlik Pricing](https://www.qlik.com/us/pricing).
- For a Qlik Anonymous Access subscription – Go to [Contact sales](https://www.qlik.com/us/try-or-buy/buy-now) and complete the application form.

A Qlik sales representative from your region will contact you. Once your account is set up, you will receive an email invitation to create your tenant.

For more information, see [*Creating and configuring your tenant*](qcs-tenant-domain.htm).

### Licensing your deployment

When you purchase a new subscription for Qlik Cloud, the license key is automatically set during the onboarding process. If you need to update your license key, contact [Qlik Support](https://community.qlik.com/t5/Support/ct-p/qlikSupport).

The tenant is licensed for use by users authorized through your corporate identity provider, which can be configured in the Administration activity center.

## Viewing subscription details

In My Qlik, the service account owner (SAO) can:

- View subscription details including the status, start and end dates, and products
- Create new tenants
- Manage OAuth clients
- Navigate to tenants for further management

For more information about subscription management, see [*Managing your Qlik Cloud subscription*](my-qlik.htm).
