# Managing your Qlik Cloud subscription

As the service account owner (SAO), you can view your subscription details in My Qlik.

## Managing different subscription types

To make changes to Qlik Cloud Analytics, Qlik Talend Cloud, or Qlik Anonymous Access subscriptions, contact your Qlik account manager.

To manage Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS or Qlik Sense Business, see [*Managing Qlik Sense Enterprise SaaS and Qlik Sense Business subscriptions*](../Admin/qcs-manage-payment.htm).

## Accessing My Qlik

You can access My Qlik in the following ways:

- Go directly to [My Qlik](https://account.myqlik.qlik.com/).
- Go to [Qlik.com](http://www.qlik.com/) and select Login > Qlik Cloud. After logging in, open the navigation menu ![Navigation menu](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_grid_menu.svg) and select My Qlik.
- If your tenant uses Qlik Account, open the navigation menu ![Navigation menu](../_images/en-US/cloud-services/Subsystems/Hub/Content/Resources/Images/Icons/ic_grid_menu.svg) in your Qlik Cloud tenant and select Administration > My Qlik.

## Viewing subscription details

To view your subscription details, sign in to My Qlik using your service account owner (SAO) credentials. You can see the subscription status, start and end dates, and included products.

Administrators can track entitlements and resource usage in the Administration activity center and the Data Capacity Reporting App.
For more information, see:

- [*Monitoring resource consumption*](../Admin/mc-monitor-consumption.htm)
- [*Monitoring detailed consumption for capacity-based subscriptions*](../Admin/usage-metrics-app.htm)
